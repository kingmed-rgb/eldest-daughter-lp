What saved you as a child is exhausting you as an adult.

You learned early that making people laugh kept you safe. Being cute got you attention. Charm got you out of trouble.

While your older siblings were trusted with responsibility, you were protected, rescued, and quietly underestimated. It worked, for a while.

Then one day you realize you're the grown woman who still calls home before every big decision, and nobody takes you as seriously as you take them.

How the workbook works

Not a book that describes you and leaves you there. Three phases, one exercise at a time.

Phase one

Awareness

Name the pattern: being treated as "the baby," the learned helplessness, the credibility gap that follows you everywhere.

Phase two

Release

Let go of charm as survival, the need for outside approval, reactive rebellion, and the resentment you've been carrying.

Phase three

Reclaiming

Step into your competence. Set boundaries. Trust your own judgment. Become the capable adult you already are.
