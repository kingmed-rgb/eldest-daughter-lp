# 02 — Voice and copy

The voice is Anna's book: an eldest daughter talking to another eldest daughter. It should be raw, warm, specific, a little funny, never clinical, never salesy. The full books are in `sources/`; when in doubt, copy the book's tone, not marketing tone.

## Hard rules (each one came from user feedback)

1. **all lowercase**, everywhere: headings, buttons, notifications, onboarding.
2. **No em dashes (—)** in user-facing copy. Use periods, commas, colons.
3. **No AI clichés.** Banned: "journey", "unlock", "empower", "transformative", "dive in", "game-changer", "it's not just X, it's Y", "you deserve" stacked three times, "safe space", "holistic", "embark", "elevate", "tapestry", "navigate", "in today's world", rhetorical triplets everywhere, "let's be honest".
4. **No guilt-tripping, ever.** The user's reaction to the line *"no guilt (you've had enough of that)"* was "this was evil writing". Do not use the reader's wound as a sales lever. No "you've sacrificed enough, now invest in yourself", no "if not now, when?", no shame for not buying, not finishing, or missing a streak.
5. **No cute quiz-show lines.** Removed: "if you checked them all? congrats, you're probably the president." Keep humor in the book's register (dry, self-aware), not memes.
6. **Honest money copy.** Say what it costs, what renews, how to cancel. No fake scarcity, no countdowns, no "only 3 left". "founding price" labels were removed.
7. **Not therapy.** Always clear that it's educational / personal development, works alongside therapy, doesn't replace it. Never diagnose ("you have eldest daughter syndrome").
8. **Role over birth order.** "this is about the role, not birth order." Don't claim birth-order science; talk about what happened in her family (parentification, being the responsible one).
9. **Spanish = natural Latin American everyday Spanish** (tú, not vos/usted; "flojera", "mandar", "literal soy yo"), proofread against the Spanish ebook, not translated word for word.

## Tone, in practice

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| "begin your healing journey today" | "do the first exercise tonight." |
| "unlock your true self" | "find out what you actually want when nobody needs anything from you." |
| "you deserve rest!" | "resting without shame" |
| "great job! 🎉 7-day streak!" | "seven days. that's seven times you chose yourself." (sparingly) |
| "you missed your streak 😢" | (nothing) or "welcome back. pick up wherever you are." |
| "Upgrade to Premium" | "keep going with the full plan" |

Notifications should be rare, quiet and opt-in. An eldest daughter does not need another person asking her for something every day.

## Key lines that work (from the landing pages)

**Hook (EN):** how many times were you told "you're so mature for your age"?
> at first, it sounded like a compliment. but for a lot of us, those words came with an unspoken contract: be responsible. be mature. and don't make life harder for anyone else.
> somewhere along the way, being needed started to feel safer than having needs of your own.

**Hook (ES):** ¿cuántas veces te dijeron "eres muy madura para tu edad"?
> al principio sonaba a cumplido. pero para muchas de nosotras, esas palabras traían un contrato que nadie dijo en voz alta: sé responsable. sé madura. y no le compliques la vida a nadie.

**The wound:** "it isn't just stress, and it isn't your personality. it's an identity shaped by duty, guilt, and invisible contracts you never agreed to sign."

**Why awareness isn't enough (the core argument for an app/workbook):**
> i could explain the wound perfectly. i'd read the posts, watched the videos, sent them to my friends with "this is literally me." and i still felt guilty every time i sat down.
> because awareness doesn't change patterns that live in your nervous system. you have to do something with it. write it down. sort it. grieve it. say no once and see what happens.

**Signs checklist (good quiz material):**
- i apologize for emotions that might inconvenience others.
- i sense tension in a room before anyone speaks.
- i've been "mature for my age" since i was 8.
- i feel guilty when i'm not productive.
- i'm everyone's therapist, but nobody's patient.
- asking for help feels like weakness.
- i secretly wish someone would take care of me.
- i confuse being loved with being useful.

Closing line after the checklist: "if you checked even one? welcome to the eldest daughter's club."

**Before → after:**
- right now: … everyone's therapist, nobody's patient
- after the workbook: your nervous system finally stands down · a body that isn't braced for the next thing · boundaries that hold, even at home · resting without shame · being loved for who you are, not what you do

**Bridge to the offer:** "you don't have to do this all at once. go at your own pace. some exercises will feel light, others will sting. that's normal. your only job is honesty, not perfection."

**Closing:** "you spent years becoming who everyone else needed. it's finally time to become you." signed "with love, anna".

**FAQ answers worth reusing in the app:**
- *i'm not technically the eldest.* "yes. this is about the role, not birth order. plenty of only children, youngest siblings, and middle kids were the responsible one: the fixer, the peacemaker, the emotional default."
- *i've done therapy and read all the books.* "because awareness and repair are two different skills. you can describe the eldest daughter wound perfectly and still re-run the pattern every day."
- *my progress resets the moment i'm around my family.* "that's exactly what the mom call drill and the financial boundaries chapter are built for: practical scripts for the moments that tend to undo people fastest."
- *do i need to finish it all at once?* "no. it's designed to go slowly. one page, one prompt, or one section, whenever you have the capacity."

**Disclaimer (keep in the app's onboarding/settings):** "this program is educational and personal-development in nature. it is not therapy, medical care, or a substitute for professional mental-health treatment." Add crisis resources in the app (it handles heavy emotions).
