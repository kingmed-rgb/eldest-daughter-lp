For the daughter who became the responsible one before she was ten.

Learn how to put down the guilt, the over-giving and the “I’m fine” reflex, without waiting for your family to change first.

In this workbook, you’ll uncover where the eldest daughter role really started, why it still runs your life as an adult, and how to finally rest without having to earn it.

You didn’t choose the weight. But you can choose to put it down.

This isn’t another book that explains the wound. It’s a workbook: you write, sort, rehearse and try things, one page at a time, until the patterns that live in your body start to loosen.

In this workbook, you’ll discover:

  • Where the role came from, and why it still runs you
  • How to tell which guilt is actually yours, and which never was
  • How to answer the anger you were never allowed to show
  • How to accept help without feeling like a burden
  • What to say on the call with mom, word for word
  • How to close the family ATM without losing the relationship
  • How to rest without inventing a reason you earned it
  • How to be loved for who you are, not for how useful you are

No matter how long you’ve been the strong one, or how “fine” you look from the outside, it isn’t too late to put it down.

The Eldest Daughter Workbook isn’t about blaming your parents, cutting off your family, or becoming someone you’re not.

Understanding the wound is the first step. Doing something with it is the step that changes your life.

This workbook was written to help you become the person you never got to be: someone who’s allowed to need, to rest, and to be cared for.
