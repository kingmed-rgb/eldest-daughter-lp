naming the moment your childhood ended can bring up many feelings, sadness, anger, even relief. but for most eldest daughters, what rises to the surface almost immediately is guilt. guilt for not doing enough. guilt for wanting rest. guilt for saying no. even guilt for being human.

that’s why the next step is tackling guilt head-on. guilt is the language of the eldest daughter wound. it’s what kept you carrying too much, for too long. before you can set it down, you need to drag it out of the shadows and see it clearly.
