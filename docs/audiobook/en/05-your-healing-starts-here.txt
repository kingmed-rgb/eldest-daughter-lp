chapter 5. your healing starts here.

<break time="1.5s" />

take a deep breath. find somewhere quiet and comfortable where you can let your guard down.

you’ve just read what the eldest daughter wound looks and feels like. maybe you saw yourself in sophia’s story. maybe you recognized your own life in the checklist. this workbook isn’t here to describe the wound, it’s here to help you move through it.

the exercises ahead are invitations go at your own pace. you don’t have to do this all at once.

some will feel light, others will sting. that’s normal.

curiosity is more powerful than judgment. notice what comes up, guilt, resistance, defensiveness, and write it down.

your only job is honesty, not perfection.

these pages are a chance to meet the eldest daughter inside you, the little girl who carried too much, too soon. some exercises will help you see her clearly. some will let you grieve what she lost. others will help you give her what she never received. and, in time, they will help you step into the woman you are becoming: someone allowed to rest, to choose, to be loved without performance.
