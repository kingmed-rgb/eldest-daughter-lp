eldest daughters don’t just carry family roles into childhood: they carry them into every relationship. you may end up parenting your partners, mothering your friends, or saying “yes” before you even know what you want.

being reliable becomes your default. but reliability without boundaries isn’t love: it’s exhaustion. this chapter will help you spot the patterns, practice the skills, and start building relationships where you’re cared for as much as you care for others.

circle the ones who: let you vent, respect your needs, offer help without strings.

the “no” practice.

this week, practice saying:

“i can’t help with that right now.”

“no, i’m not available.”

“that doesn’t work for me.”

being loved isn’t the same as being needed. boundaries don’t push people away: they show you who is capable of loving you as you are. the more you practice them, the more you’ll attract relationships where you’re cared for, not just leaned on.
