healing your relationship with your parents is only half the story. because while you were carrying their wounds, you were also carrying your siblings. you became their third parent, their shield, their fixer. and while they got a childhood, you often got a job.

this chapter is about resetting that dynamic. about grieving what you missed, naming the resentment, and finally stepping back into the role you were meant to have: just being their sister.

remember this...

you are not your siblings’ parent. you are their sister. stepping out of the “third parent” role doesn’t abandon them, it gives both of you the chance to build a real relationship.

and once you’ve put down the weight of parenting your parents and your siblings, you might find yourself asking:

“now what? who am i without all the responsibility?”

that’s where we end: not with duty, not with fixing, but with joy. with laughter. with play. with the little girl inside you who never stopped waiting for her turn.
